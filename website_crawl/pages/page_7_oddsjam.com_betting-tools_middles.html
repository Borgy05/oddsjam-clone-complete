HOW DOES IT WORK?

Pick up rare opportunities to bet both sides of a game and win both bets on an over/under, or spread bet.

These are essentially arbitrage bets where you also have a slim chance to win both sides of a bet.

Imagine over 51.5 points for an NFL game is +104 with DraftKings sportsbook, but the under 52.5 is available at +101 with Fanduel sportsbook. If you wager $100 on both sides of this bet, you would win at least one side... additionally, if exactly 52 points are scored, you would win both bets.
